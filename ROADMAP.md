# Roadmap

## v0.3.1 — Quality policy and maintenance

- retire the unused external reviewer proposal without activating Quality Judge scoring;
- keep the visible preview separate from canonical score, winner, verdict, and battle identity;
- make request telemetry current-invocation truthful and cache-invariant;
- reconcile actual request calls in the required benchmark;
- refresh reviewed build tools and group routine dependency maintenance.

## v0.3.0 — Quality Judge Preview

- parser-backed, bounded quality readings separate from canonical scoring;
- maintained-codebase and publicly attributed readings kept distinct;
- preview-only policy and a disabled external reviewer proposal, later retired for v0.3.1;
- complete public contributor, platform, security, and package gates.

## Later — quality hardening

Expand safe parser coverage, reduce false positives and false negatives through synthetic
fixtures, and keep request accounting and result identity cache-invariant. Canonical scoring
remains a separately versioned product decision.
