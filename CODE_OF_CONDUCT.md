# Contributor Covenant 3.0 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights,
and contributions of all individuals, regardless of characteristics including race, ethnicity,
caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender
identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social
origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status. The same privileges of
participation are extended to everyone who participates in good faith and in accordance with
this Covenant.

## Encouraged Behaviors

While acknowledging differences in social norms, we strive to meet our community's expectations
for positive behavior. We understand that words and actions may be interpreted differently based
on culture, background, or native language. We agree to:

1. Respect the purpose of our community and its ways of gathering.
2. Engage kindly and honestly.
3. Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Take responsibility for our actions and contributions.
5. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
6. Commit to repairing harm when it occurs.
7. Promote and sustain the well-being of the community.

## Restricted Behaviors

Instances, threats, and promotion of these behaviors violate this Code of Conduct:

1. Harassment, including violating expressed boundaries or continuing unnecessary personal
   attention after a clear request to stop.
2. Character attacks through insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments.
3. Stereotyping or discrimination based on immutable identities or traits.
4. Sexualization inappropriate to the context or purpose of the community.
5. Violating confidentiality by sharing or acting on private information without permission.
6. Endangerment through causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm.
7. Other behavior that threatens the well-being of the community.

Other restricted behavior includes misleading identity, failing to credit sources, sharing
promotional material outside community norms, and irresponsibly presenting material that
includes or promotes another restricted behavior.

## Reporting an Issue

Report a possible violation privately to the repository owner using the public private-contact
channel listed on [@gavinjudd's GitHub profile](https://github.com/gavinjudd). If no suitable
private project channel is available, use [GitHub's confidential abuse-reporting
process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Do not open a public issue or include
credentials, private source, or unnecessary personal information.

Community moderators take reports seriously, investigate them privately, prioritize safety and
confidentiality, and make enforcement as transparent as those priorities permit. Not every
conflict is a violation; encouraged behaviors should also be used to prevent and resolve tension.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

If an investigation finds a violation, moderators may use this ladder according to impact and
severity, skipping lower levels when necessary:

1. **Warning.** A private written warning, acknowledgment of responsibility, clarification of
   expectations, and appropriate repair such as an apology.
2. **Temporarily limited activities.** A time-limited cooldown or interaction restriction after
   repetition or a more serious first incident, with clear conditions for return.
3. **Temporary suspension.** Removal from community spaces for a defined period after repeated or
   serious violations, with explicit return conditions.
4. **Permanent ban.** Removal from all community spaces when other measures failed or the
   violation makes continued participation unsafe.

This ladder is guidance and does not remove moderator discretion in the community's best interest.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces and when an individual officially represents
the community in public or elsewhere.
